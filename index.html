Why M&C

You didn't move here for this.

Your daughter is putting on weight and you know why. There's nowhere for her to run. The compound is a parking lot. The road outside is not a road for children. So she stays inside, on the sofa, on the phone, and you tell yourself it's fine for now.

You leave the house at six and you're still in traffic at eight. You pass the gym on the way home. You've paid for it for four months. You've been twice. It's not laziness. It's that by the time you reach it, there is nothing left of you.

A trip to buy milk and bread eats your Saturday.

And there's that stretch between the main road and your gate. Poorly lit. Too long. You walk it fast, keys in your hand, phone in your pocket, checking behind you. Every single evening. You've stopped calling it fear. You call it “just being careful.”

Then there was the cat. Or the dog. The landlord said no pets, after you'd already paid deposit, and you spent a week finding someone to take her. Your son still asks about her.

None of this was on the listing. The listing said spacious 2 bedroom, ample parking, borehole water.

That's not luxury. That's the bare minimum, and it exists. Most people just don't know where to look.

Privacy Policy

How M&C Home Listings handles the information you share with us. Last updated August 2026.

What we collect

When you book a viewing or send a landlord enquiry we collect the name, phone number and any notes you type into the form. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we hold that conversation and the number or address it came from.

How we use it

Only to respond to you: to confirm a viewing, answer a question about a property, or discuss managing your property. We do not sell, rent or share your details with third parties for marketing. Where a viewing requires it, we share your first name and arrival time with the caretaker or landlord of that building, and nothing more.

How forms work

Our forms do not send data to a server of ours. They open WhatsApp with a message drafted for you, and nothing is transmitted until you press send in WhatsApp. Messages you send there are covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand which listings people view and how visitors find us. It sets cookies and records anonymised usage such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. It does not tell us who you are. You can block these cookies in your browser without affecting how the site works.

How long we keep it

Enquiry messages are kept for as long as we are actively helping you, and for up to two years afterwards so we can pick up where we left off. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019 you may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email homelistingsmc@gmail.com and we will respond within seven days.
