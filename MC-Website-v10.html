Common questions

FAQ

How do you verify a listing?

We check the title, confirm the real owner, and visit the house in person before it ever appears on our books.

Do you charge a fee?

We charge a small fee for viewing houses. We will always tell you the exact amount up front, before anything is arranged, so there are never any surprises.

I am abroad. Can I view remotely?

Yes. We run live video viewings over WhatsApp or a call, and can send you a full recording plus verified documents.

Do you rent and sell?

Both. We rent out houses and we sell them, including maisonettes, townhouses and standalone homes. Whether you want a place for a year or somewhere to buy and keep, we can help.

I am a landlord. Can you manage my house?

Yes. Head to the For Landlords page and send us the details, and we will come back to you with what we think your house will rent for.
